## Implementation notes

- The 6 P0 findings concentrate in two files: `app/(admin)/admin/actions.ts` (2) and `app/api/onboarding/entrepreneur/route.ts` (2). A single targeted PR can land most of the P0 work.
- The "monotone-up" sync shipped in commit `5fa1e42` is correct for the steady state but races on simultaneous patches (LC-15) — fixable with `setProgressMap(prev => ...)`.
- דוד's 4 acceleration fixes from commit `3a3473a` are intact; the QA found 2 *new* issues introduced by those changes (DA-1 maxDuration on Hobby + DA-3 optimistic fact-check 75) — both addressable in the same follow-up PR.
